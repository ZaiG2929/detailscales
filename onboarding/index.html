03✓

Send your customer and lead list

This is the step that matters most. The fastest money in the whole system comes from people who already paid you or already asked for a price.

10 to 20 min
Messy is fine. Missing is not. A spreadsheet with half the emails blank is worth more than a clean one you never get round to sending. We clean it, dedupe it and sort it. Send what you have.

What to send

  • Past customers. Anyone who has paid you for a wash, detail or coating
  • Unclosed quotes. Anyone who asked for a price and never booked
  • Coating customers with the date it went on, for maintenance reminders
  • Name and phone at minimum. Email, vehicle, last service and date if you have them

Where it probably lives

  • Booking software: the Customers or Clients list usually has an Export or Download CSV option
  • iPhone contacts: icloud.com, Contacts, select all, then Export vCard
  • Android or Gmail: contacts.google.com, then Export, then Google CSV
  • Instagram DMs and texts: screenshots of the conversations that never booked are fine
  • Invoices or a notebook: photos work. We will type it up

Where to send it

Use the upload link rather than email or text where you can. It is your customers' contact details, so it should travel the private way.

Your list is only ever used to message your own customers, on your behalf, in wording you approve before anything goes out.

Our side

What we build while you do that. In this order, on purpose.

Most agencies start with ads. We start with the revenue you already have, so the ads land on a system that catches what they bring in.

  1. Days 1 to 3

    Your list, cleaned. Your messages, written.

    We import and dedupe your list, split it into unclosed quotes, past customers and coating owners, and write the recovery and reactivation messages. You see and approve every word before a single one sends. Business texting registration is submitted the day we have your details. Carriers can take a few days to clear it.

  2. Days 3 to 7

    Dead leads and past customers get worked.

    Dead lead recovery goes live, then reactivation to your past detail, wash and coating customers. Review and referral requests switch on after every finished job, with coating maintenance reminders behind them. This is usually where the first recovered jobs come from.

  3. Days 7 to 10

    Every enquiry answered inside 60 seconds.

    The AI voice agent is trained on your prices, your area and the objections you gave us, then connected to your calendar. We send a real test enquiry end to end and you hear exactly what your customers will hear before it goes live.

  4. Days 10 to 14

    Paid ads, switched on last.

    Coating and detailing campaigns go live at the ad spend we agreed on the kickoff call, into a system that now follows up on every click.

  5. Every week after

    A plain English report.

    Jobs booked, revenue produced, cost per booked job, and what we are changing next. Not a dashboard you never open.

Onboarding questions

The things new clients ask first.

My customer list is a mess. Should I tidy it first?

No. Send it as it is. Tidying it is our job, and every day it sits on your laptop is a day the unclosed quotes in it get colder.

I don't really have a list. Most of it is in my phone.

That is the most common answer we get. Export your phone contacts using the steps in step three, and send screenshots of any DM or text threads where someone asked for a price. That is a list.

Won't texting old customers annoy them?

These are people who already paid you or asked you for a price. The messages read like they came from you, they are sent at sensible hours, and anyone who replies stop is removed automatically. You approve the wording before anything sends.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Customers keep calling the number they already have. We go through how the voice agent picks up missed and after-hours calls on the kickoff call.

When do I start paying for ads?

Not until the follow-up is live, usually in the second week. Ad spend is billed by Meta or Google straight to your card, never through us.

What if I get stuck on a step?

Skip it, finish the rest, and bring it to the kickoff call. A half-finished step four is far better than steps three and five waiting on it.

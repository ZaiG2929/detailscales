Before you ask

The four questions every owner asks. Answered now.

Each one is a short video. The written answer sits under it if you'd rather read.

01

What happens on the call, and what should I bring?

We go through your last 90 days: how many people asked for a price, how many you quoted, how many booked, and how big your past customer list is. From that we show you where the money is leaking and what your first 90 days with us would look like.

Bring three rough numbers. Guesses are fine.

  • Inquiries a month: calls, DMs and forms.
  • Your coating price, and what a regular detail goes for.
  • Where your customer list lives, and about how many names are on it.

If you're not a fit, we'll say so on the call. No pressure to sign anything that day.

02

What have you done for other detailers?

We've built this system for other detailing shops. In this video I walk you through what we set up for them, what it brought in, and how fast.

03

How is this different from every agency that's pitched me?

Most agencies start with ads. We start with the money you already have: the people who asked for a quote and never heard back, and the customers who already paid you once.

  • Unclosed quotes get worked first. Fastest cash in the system.
  • Past customers get brought back by text.
  • Every new inquiry gets a call back inside 60 seconds.
  • Ads go on last, once the follow-up exists to catch what they bring in.

And we only work with detailers.

04

How does the guarantee work, and how does pricing work?

If the system doesn't add $10,000 in revenue within 90 days, your fee is refunded and we keep working for free until it does.

It has conditions, because a guarantee only holds if both sides do their part: your customer list, access to your accounts, replies to booked jobs within 24 hours, room in your calendar, and the agreed ad spend.

Pricing depends on your shop and which parts of the system you need, so we set it on the call once we've seen your numbers. You'll know the exact figure before anything is signed. Ad spend is paid straight to Meta, never through us.
